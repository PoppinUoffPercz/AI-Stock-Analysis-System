# Famous Contrarian Investors

> **Created:** 2026-07-06
> **Purpose:** Profiles of notable contrarian value investors for reference and strategy comparison

---

## 1. Michael Burry

- **Fund:** Scion Asset Management
- **Style:** Deep value / contrarian / "ick" investing
- **Known For:** Subprime mortgage CDS short (2005-2007)
- **Key Strategy:** Buy roadkill stocks within 10-15% of 52W low with strong fundamentals
- **Current Focus:** Short AI bubble (PLTR/NVDA puts Q3 2025), long beaten-down pharma/energy
- **See:** Michael Burry Methodology

---

## 2. David Tepper

- **Fund:** Appaloosa Management (founded 1993)
- **Style:** Distressed debt, contrarian macro
- **Known For:** Buying distressed bank debt during 2009 financial crisis for massive gains
- **Key Trade:** Bought Bank of America preferred shares at $3-5 during 2009 crash, later exchanged to common at ~$15 (300%+ gain)
- **Strategy:** "Wait for the panic, then buy the most-affected quality assets"
- **Returns:** ~25% net annual returns since 1993
- **Current Focus:** Various equities, often concentrated in single sectors

---

## 3. Seth Klarman

- **Fund:** Baupost Group (founded 1982)
- **Style:** Deep value, absolute return, event-driven
- **Known For:** Margin of Safety (book that sells for $1,500+), rarely loses money
- **Key Strategy:** Look for "cigar butts" — one puff left, but it's a PROFITABLE puff. Focus on downside protection first.
- **Distinctive Rule:** Will hold up to 40% cash if no great opportunities exist
- **Returns:** ~20% annual return since 1982, rarely has a down year
- **Key Quote:** *"Margin of safety is the secret of sound investing."*

---

## 4. Howard Marks

- **Fund:** Oaktree Capital Management
- **Style:** Distressed debt, contrarian macro
- **Known For:** Memo writing, market cycle awareness
- **Key Strategy:** "Second level thinking" — what does the market believe that is wrong?
- **Key Quote:** *"Being too far ahead of your time is indistinguishable from being wrong."*
- **Returns:** Oaktree distressed debt funds ~15%+ annualized
- **Cycle Framework:** Tracks where we are in the market cycle (euphoria vs capitulation) and adjusts risk accordingly

---

## 5. John Paulson

- **Fund:** Paulson & Co.
- **Style:** Merger arbitrage, event-driven, macro shorts
- **Known For:** Largest ever single trade profit ($15B+) shorting subprime mortgages via CDS in 2007-2008
- **Key Trade:** Bought CDS on mortgage-backed securities 2006, held through 2007-2008 crash
- **Strategy:** Identify systemic imbalances and bet against them asymmetrically (limited downside via CDS premium, massive upside on default)
- **Returns:** 2007 fund +590%, but later years had some large losses — cautionary tale of strategy drift

---

## 6. Stanley Druckenmiller

- **Fund:** Duquesne Capital (1981-2000), now family office
- **Style:** Macro, top-down with bottom-up confirmation
- **Key Trade:** Short USD / long German mark in late 1980s; long equities through 1990s
- **Strategy:** Identify the biggest macro imbalance in the world and bet against it. Shifts asset class aggressively when thesis changes.
- **Legendary Stat:** Never had a down year in 30 years
- **Returns:** ~30% annual returns across 3 decades
- **Key Quote:** *"It's not whether you're right or wrong, but how much money you make when you're right and how much you lose when you're wrong."*

---

## 7. Carl Icahn

- **Style:** Activist investor, contrarian value
- **Known For:** Hostile takeovers and activist campaigns (TWA, Texaco, RJR Nabisco, Xerox)
- **Strategy:** Buy large stakes in underperforming companies, push for asset sales/management changes/spin-offs, exit after catalyst
- **Key Pattern:** Buy at discount, force the value to be realized
- **Returns:** Icahn Enterprises ~12-15% annualized (highly volatile)

---

## 8. Bruce Berkowitz

- **Fund:** Fairholme Funds
- **Style:** Concentrated contrarian value, deep dive on few names
- **Known For:** Holding 20+ positions, big 5-10% allocation per name
- **Key Trade:** Large positions in financials during 2009 (BAC, AIG) for massive recovery gains
- **Strategy:** Extreme concentration thesis on misunderstood, distressed financials
- **Cautionary Tale:** Some underperformance in 2010s when thesis didn't materialize (Sears, Florida real estate trusts)

---

## 9. John Templeton

- **Fund:** Templeton Growth Fund (founded 1954)
- **Style:** Global contrarian, low-P/E investing
- **Known For:** Bought 100 stocks trading below $1 during WWII, made many times his money
- **Key Strategy:** Maximum pessimism buying — "invest at the point of maximum pessimism"
- **Golden Rule:** "Bull markets are born on pessimism, grow on skepticism, mature on optimism, and die on euphoria."
- **Returns:** ~15% annual for 50+ years

---

## 10. Prem Watsa

- **Fund:** Fairfax Financial Holdings
- **Style:** Deep value, contrarian insurance-focused
- **Known For:** "Canada's Warren Buffett", long-shot bets including deflation hedges
- **Strategy:** Buy cash-rich, distressed insurance businesses; invest the float in contrarian value equities
- **Returns:** ~15-18% annualized over decades
- **Current Patterns:** Often takes significant derivative positions based on macro view

## Common Threads Among Contrarians
1. **Patience** — Willing to wait years for the thesis to play out
2. **Conviction Despite Pain** — Buy when everyone else is selling
3. **Margin of Safety First** — Always protected against permanent loss
4. **Active Reassessment** — Willing to change thesis with new information
5. **Avoid Crowded Trades** — Popularity destroys future returns
6. **Asymmetric Risk/Reward** — Limited downside, large upside when right
7. **Cash Is a Position** — Not always fully invested
8. **Intellectual Honesty** — Quick to admit mistakes and cut losses
